What this looks like in action

We're proud to be building a new pathway into AI careers for New Yorkers.

Zero Irving × Skillcrush by PowerToFly
Break Into Tech: AI Developer Scholarship | New York City

This September, 15 New Yorkers will begin a journey from complete beginner to AI Developer.

Fully funded through the Zero Irving Workforce Development Project Fund, the program was created for New Yorkers historically underrepresented in tech — people with the ambition and potential to build a new career, but who may not have had access to the traditional pathways into the industry.

The challenge

AI is creating new opportunities at extraordinary speed. But access to those opportunities isn't equally distributed.

The challenge was bigger than providing a course.

It was to create a pathway that gives people historically underrepresented in tech the skills, tools, support and career preparation to participate in this new economy.

What we built

We designed the full learner journey — from recruitment and selection through technical training, wraparound support and career readiness.

Over six months, learners move from foundational web development through full-stack development and AI application development.

And because access means more than tuition, the program includes the supports learners need to participate and progress:

A laptop Transportation support Live instruction Mentorship and community Ongoing learner support Up to six months of career coaching
