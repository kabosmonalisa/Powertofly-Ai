Two days, built to work together

Day 1 · Wednesday, February 3

The Summit

Settle in for a couple of hours of panels and fireside chats with Black leaders who are shaping their industries. They'll share how they built their careers, what leadership has taught them, how they open doors for the people coming up behind them, and the advice they wish they'd heard earlier.

We'll close with a friendly prep session for the job fair. We'll help you get comfortable introducing yourself, think through what you'd like to ask, and feel ready to make the most of the next day.

Day 2 · Thursday, February 4

The Virtual Job Fair

The job fair is where everything comes together. We'll start on the main stage, where each participating company introduces itself and joins a roundtable on amplifying Black excellence, their approach to inclusion, and what it's like to work on their teams. It's a relaxed way to get to know a company and picture yourself there.

Then you'll join breakout rooms with recruiters and hiring managers who are glad to answer your questions and share advice you can put to use right away, including:

  • Tips for resumes and applications
  • How to prepare for interviews, from the first screen to the final round
  • Open roles and what their teams are looking for
  • The technology and tools their teams use every day
Join the job fair live

We'd love to see you live

Both days are recorded, so you can catch up anytime. If you can, we hope you'll join the job fair live. It's the best way to take part in the conversation, and we let our hiring partners know who attended live. That's a nice boost if you're looking for your next opportunity.
