New AI branding for the Stripe checkout

On Stripe's hosted Checkout you control the panel background color and the logo — nothing else. So the rebrand is one honest move: coral → ink, and a crisp logo. Everything else in the frame is Stripe's own chrome.

Background → set brand color to #0A0A0A (--ink). Solid only; Stripe can't take a gradient.
Logo → upload a crisp white AI lockup. The current asset is low-res (1.8 KB PNG) — export it from vector at 2–3×.
