Beyond the résumé: building human-first AI for fairer access to opportunity

Ever feel like your résumé barely scratches the surface of what you can actually do?

Colette Hart Co-founder & CEO, Idonea
August 6, 2026 1:00 – 2:00 PM ET Virtual Free to attend
Register now → Meet the speaker

About this session

This session digs into a bold rethink of how hiring works, led by Colette Hart, co-founder and CEO of Idonea and a human-centered AI systems architect with nearly 30 years of experience across UX, product design, and digital strategy. Colette will unpack how AI can be built to reveal real capability — not just keywords and credentials — while keeping people in control of their own information every step of the way. If you've ever wondered what fair, transparent hiring technology could actually look like, this is one you won't want to miss.

What will happen

Here's what you'll take away from the hour

See past the résumé

Why résumés and keyword matching often miss the full picture of a person's capability — and what a more evidence-based alternative can look like.

Reduce bias early

How candidate-controlled privacy and identity release can reduce bias early in the hiring process.

Know what to look for

What to look for when evaluating whether an AI hiring system is built with consent, transparency, and real human oversight in mind.
