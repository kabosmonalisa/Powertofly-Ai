Talent Network

Two journeys, one manual middle.

Candidates apply to join a talent community and wait for review. Clients open a private cohort, shortlist anonymized profiles, and ask for introductions. In version one, everything between the two sides is our team, working in email and HubSpot.

Candidate

Apply to join a community

Reuses the job application flow. A community is posted like a role, so applying feels like something people already know how to do.

01

Find the community

"Apply to join the AI Product Talent Network" is posted like a job — on the job board, in the newsletter, and shared by the team.

02

Start the application

Name, work email, resume, LinkedIn. The same first step as every other application on PowerToFly.

03

Answer the screening questions

A short set owned per community: focus area, years of experience, tools, availability, rate, work authorization.

04

Submit for review

The confirmation is honest about the gate: nothing is live yet, a human reads it, and here is roughly when you will hear back.

05

We review — by hand

The team approves, holds for a later batch, or declines. No automated scoring in v1.

06

Accepted into the batch

An email confirms it, and the anonymized version of the profile becomes available to put in front of clients.

Client

Open a cohort, ask for an introduction

A private page per client, per cohort. No dashboard, no account, no contact details — the introduction has to come through us.

01

Get the link

After a scoping call we email one private cohort link with an access code. It belongs to that client and that batch.

02

Unlock the cohort

Work email plus the code from the email. Nothing to create, nothing to remember.

03

Review anonymized profiles

Role, seniority, years, region, skills, availability, rate. No name, no photo, no current employer.

04

Shortlist

Tick the people worth meeting. The count follows down the page so the ask is always one click away.

05

Request introductions — or send hiring needs

One short form: who you picked, the role, the timeline. If nobody fits, the same form takes the brief instead.

06

We introduce — by hand

The team emails both sides, gets consent from the candidate, and logs the whole thread in HubSpot.

Internal · no screens in v1

What the team does between the two sides

Naming this is the point of version one. Every step below is a person in an inbox, not a feature — which is what keeps the first release small.

01

Work the review queue

Read each application, decide accept / hold / decline, and send the outcome email.

02

Anonymize the profile

Strip name, photo, and employer; keep what a client needs to judge. This is a judgement call today, not a rule the system enforces.

03

Assemble the cohort and send the link

Pick who goes in front of which client, then email the private link and code.

04

Make the introduction and follow up

Ask the candidate first, introduce both sides, and keep the deal moving in HubSpot.
