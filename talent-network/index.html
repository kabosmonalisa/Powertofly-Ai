How joining works

Every application is read by a person. Here is the whole path, including the part where you wait.

01

Apply

Your details, your resume, and your LinkedIn — the same application you would fill in for a role. About five minutes.

02

Answer the screening questions

What you have shipped with AI, the tools you use, your availability and your rate. Short answers are fine.

03

We review

Our team reads every application. You hear back either way, usually within two weeks.

04

You are in the batch

Accepted members go in front of companies we are actively hiring for. We ask before we ever share your name.
